    Build To Scale: The Art of Sales Engineering¶

    graph TD A[Sales Engineering] --> B[Technical Expertise] A --> C[Customer Understanding] A --> D[Innovation Models] B --> E[Product Knowledge] C --> F[Jobs To Be Done] D --> G[Disruptive Innovation]

    The Super Power of Solution Engineers¶

    "The SE is like the mechanic who pulls you aside and tells you to record the dates of your oil changes so there's no risk that a warranty claim will be rejected."
    — Zack Urlocker, Build To Scale, Nov 12, 2024

    Building Trust Through Expertise¶

    A successful Sales Engineer (SE) establishes trust through:

    1. Deep Product Knowledge
    2. Understanding technical specifications
    3. Mastering product capabilities

    4. Knowing implementation details

    5. Client Situation Understanding

    6. Analyzing customer needs
    7. Identifying pain points
    8. Proposing tailored solutions

    Best Practices¶

    1. Technical Discovery¶

    • Conduct thorough technical assessments
    • Document customer requirements
    • Identify integration points

    2. Solution Design¶

    • Create proof-of-concept implementations
    • Develop technical specifications
    • Plan implementation roadmaps

    3. Customer Success¶

    • Guide successful implementations
    • Provide technical expertise
    • Ensure customer satisfaction

    Integration Points¶

    With Constructor Theory¶

    • Analyzing feasible transformations

    • Identifying solution spaces

    With Platform Engineering¶

    • Designing scalable solutions
    • Implementing robust architectures
    • Ensuring operational efficiency

    Conclusion¶

    Sales Engineering combines technical expertise with customer understanding to: - Build lasting trust - Drive successful implementations - Enable business transformation

    This approach aligns with Jobs to Be Done theory and disruptive innovation principles, creating value through deep technical and business understanding.
