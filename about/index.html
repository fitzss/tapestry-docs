About

The Project

The Tapestry of Possibility unifies diverse domains through a shared focus on tasks, constraints, and feasibility. Whether examining robotic manipulation, cloud computing, or financial transactions, we ask: "Which transformations are possible, under what constraints, and how can we modify these constraints to achieve new possibilities?"

Core Strands

The project weaves together four innovative strands:

  1. Constructor Theory & Counterfactuals: Examining what could or couldn't happen and why
  2. Physical AI: Intelligent machines operating under real-world constraints
  3. Serverless & Platform Engineering: Declarative, event-driven computing
  4. Decentralized Finance: Contract-driven financial transformations

Vision

Our aim is to shift multiple disciplines toward a unified methodology that:

  • Fosters adaptability and innovation
  • Enables systematic problem-solving
  • Supports decentralized systems
  • Promotes knowledge-driven transformation
